MY STORY

Driven by Curiosity, Grounded in Engineering

From my early years in Souk El Tennine, I was captivated by technology—not merely as consumer tools, but as evolving systems with the capacity to solve tangible everyday problems and make human life simpler.

During high school, while specializing in Technique Mathématiques — Génie Électrique, I decided to take programming seriously. I dove headfirst into Python through Angela Yu's 100 Days of Code, completing daily project challenges across data structures, desktop GUIs, browser automation, and Flask web servers.

Now as a student at École Nationale Polytechnique d'Alger (ENP), I combine rigorous mathematical foundations with continuous software development, actively learning Flutter to create clean, responsive mobile applications that will eventually integrate Python and AI backend systems.
