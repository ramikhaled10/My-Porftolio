About Me

A little bit about me and my journey

My name is Rami Khaled. I'm an 18-year-old engineering student from Algeria who loves coding and building useful things.

I was born and raised in Algeria, and I went to school in Souk El Tennine through primary, middle, and high school. Ever since I was young, I've been really curious about computers and technology. What caught my attention wasn't just writing code, but how software can take something complicated or repetitive and make people's lives easier.

Right now, I am in my first year at École Nationale Polytechnique d'Alger (ENP). Back in high school, I decided to start learning programming seriously. I began with Python. My first big step was taking Angela Yu's 100 Days of Code Python course.

I started learning Python to build a strong foundation in programming. My goal wasn't just to become a Python developer — I wanted to understand software fundamentals really well so that I could later go deeper into Artificial Intelligence.

Right now, alongside my classes at ENP, I am learning Flutter and Dart for mobile development, and I am taking an online course from EDUCBA through Coursera.
